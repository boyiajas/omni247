## Full Description

Omni247 helps communities report incidents, share local alerts, and stay informed through a live safety map.

With Omni247, users can:

- Report incidents with exact map locations
- Upload photos or videos as supporting evidence
- Browse nearby reports and community alerts
- Follow updates as reports are reviewed and verified
- Control report privacy with public, anonymous, or private options
- Receive notifications about important activity and safety events

Omni247 is designed for real-world reporting across safety, emergencies, infrastructure issues, public events, and environmental concerns. The app gives users a simple way to document what is happening around them and make that information visible to the right audience.

Key features:

- Interactive map view for incident discovery
- Category-based reporting
- Photo and video attachments
- Location-based reporting
- Real-time notifications and alerts
- Privacy controls for user reports

Privacy and safety:

- Omni247 uses location to place reports accurately on the map
- Media uploads are user-initiated and attached to reports
- Users can manage privacy settings inside the app
- Account deletion requests can be submitted at `https://omni-247.com/account-deletion`

Whether you are reporting an urgent incident or monitoring what is happening nearby, Omni247 gives you a faster way to capture and share community intelligence.
